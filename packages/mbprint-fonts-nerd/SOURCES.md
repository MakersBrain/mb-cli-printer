# Font sources

The four bundled faces and `OFL.txt` were extracted from the official
[Nerd Fonts v3.5.1 JetBrainsMono release](https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/releases/tag/v3.5.1).

Only the `JetBrainsMono Nerd Font` family is included. Mono and proportional
variants, alternate weights, and the no-ligature family are intentionally
omitted to keep this optional wheel reasonably sized.
